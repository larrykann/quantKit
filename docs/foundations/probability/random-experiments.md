# Random Experiments

A random experiment is any activity with uncertain outcomes that can be repeated under similar conditions. The key characteristic is that while the procedure is reproducible, the specific outcome cannot be predicted with certainty. Examples include coin tosses, dice rolls, or capturing stock market data over time.

**Financial Examples:**

- "Capture daily closing prices for AAPL stock"
- "Measure monthly portfolio returns" 
- "Record daily trading volumes"
- "Observe interest rate changes"

The repeatability under similar conditions is crucial - we can perform the same type of measurement or observation multiple times, but each instance may yield different results due to the inherent uncertainty in the system being observed.
